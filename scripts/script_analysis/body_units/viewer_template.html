Devanagari body units

A body is one pen path. I cut each real body into units wherever the curve changes direction of turn or goes straight, and mark sharp corners with a dot. Read from where the body attaches outward. The second set is new bodies chained from the same kinds of units, drawn on a stem or a short descender.

Real bodies

Each card names the letter and its model stroke. Under the drawing: kind, length and turn in degrees of every unit, in order.
