Devanagari stroke viewer

Each card shows the Unicode letter, its romanisation, and the stroke form from the stroke-graph model. The second set is new letters from Generator v2. Select a card for its junction counts.

Real letters

Traced from your drawings. The letters with a 3 shape (a, ā, o, au) and tha, dha, bha have no purple bar of their own.

Generated letters

New letters built from the same strokes and junction rules. Each card names its style configuration and structure key (bar, right side, body, variant).
