Delete your Conlang Forge account

In the app (Android)

  1. Open the Languages tab.
  2. Tap Delete account under your email address.
  3. Enter your password (or, if you only use Google sign-in, type your email address) and confirm.

On the website

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Scroll to Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

If you cannot sign in

Email splandorf-support@googlegroups.com from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my account", and it will be deleted.

What is deleted

Your account, sign-in records, and every language saved under it are removed permanently and cannot be recovered. Anonymous usage counts with no link to you are kept for cost accounting. See the privacy policy.
