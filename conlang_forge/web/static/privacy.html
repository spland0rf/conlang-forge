Privacy policy

Conlang Forge, last updated 13 October 2026. Operated by an individual developer. Contact: splandorf@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a display name, and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address and name; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Your content: the languages you create, their settings, and any sample text you supply to shape a language.
  • Text you translate: English or constructed-language text you submit to the translator.
  • Usage and security records: how many model tokens you use, sign-in times, and a log of security-relevant actions such as password changes.

How it is used

To run the service: sign you in, store and show your languages, translate text, enforce usage limits, and send account emails (confirm your address, reset your password). We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Who else sees it

  • AI provider: when you translate or analyse text, that text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be processed. Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google: only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
  • Email delivery: a mail-sending service delivers account emails to your address.
  • Hosting: the service runs on a server in Google Cloud.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and languages until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time: in the app, choose "Delete account"; on the website, open Account and choose "Delete account". This permanently removes your account, sign-in records and every language saved under it. Anonymous usage counts (with no link back to you) are kept for cost accounting. See how to delete your account, including if you can no longer sign in.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. The mobile app keeps your sign-in in the phone's secure storage.

Children

Conlang Forge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or requests: splandorf@gmail.com.
