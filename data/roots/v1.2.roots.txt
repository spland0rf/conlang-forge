# conlang-forge root map for vocab v1.2  (DRAFT for human review)
#
# Three kinds of shared root:
#   1. Conceptual families  (family lines) - related roots sharing an opening sound. Normally at most 5 roots per family (6 only with very strong affinity).
#   2. Grammatical derivation (word = base +REL)  - uncapped; built algorithmically.
#   3. Compounds (word = a | b)                   - uncapped; built algorithmically.
#
#   word = base +REL [+REL ...]     derive: base word plus regular affix(es), applied left to right
#   word = a | b [| c] [+REL]       compound of existing words (head-final); optional affixes after
#   @name                           a bound root: a root that is not itself a dictionary word
#   family NAME: w1 w2 w3 ...       related, non-derivable roots that share an opening sound
#   siblings GROUP: fam1 fam2 ...   families that were one natural group too big for the cap; the generator
#                                   gives them related (not identical) sounds
# Any vocab word not defined below is its own free root.
